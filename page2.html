About TechNova Solutions

A passionate team dedicated to turning complex technology challenges into simple, powerful solutions.

Our Story

TechNova Solutions was founded in 2010 by a small team of engineers who believed that great technology should be accessible to every business — not just the Fortune 500.

What started as a two-person consultancy in a garage has grown into a 120-person organisation serving clients across North America, Europe, and Asia. We've shipped over 500 successful projects, ranging from consumer mobile apps to enterprise-grade cloud platforms.

Our culture is built on curiosity, craftsmanship, and accountability. Every line of code we write and every design decision we make is guided by one question: "Does this genuinely help our client?"
